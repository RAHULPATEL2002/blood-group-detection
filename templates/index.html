Best capture tips

Get a clean infrared scan

  • Ensure the hand is fully visible and flat.
  • Keep distance consistent with the infrared sensor.
  • Avoid glare, reflections, or metal accessories.
  • Allow 10 seconds for temperature to stabilize.

How it works

  1. Infrared image is resized and normalized.
  2. VGG16 classifier evaluates learned heat patterns.
  3. Top blood group probabilities are returned instantly.
